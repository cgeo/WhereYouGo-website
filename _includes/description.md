**WhereYouGo has been discontinued**

WhereYouGo is no longer maintained and has been discontinued due to limited resources and evolving technical requirements.

The good news is that full Wherigo support is now available in [c:geo](https://www.cgeo.org/), giving you an even better experience with modern offline maps, translations and seamless integration with Geocaching.

Learn more at [www.cgeo.org](https://www.cgeo.org/).
